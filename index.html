About Barmentech LLC

Barmentech LLC is a Wyoming-registered software development company specializing in SaaS solutions for businesses across the Americas. We combine cutting-edge technology with deep understanding of both US and Latin American markets.

Our team delivers secure, scalable, and compliant software solutions using industry-standard technologies including TypeScript, NestJS, Next.js, Python, and PostgreSQL. All projects include proper security implementations, multi-tenant architecture, and cloud deployment with CI/CD pipelines.
